## I. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & UTILITY PATENT CLAIMS

### 1. Synchronized Broadcast-to-Mobile Audience Second-Screen Architecture
- **Connected TV Master Broadcast Pipeline**: Master live video feed transmitted over broadcast television, connected TV apps (Roku, Apple TV, Android TV, FireTV), and streaming protocols (YouTube Live, Twitch, Kick).
- **Dynamic Session Ingestion via Dynamic On-Screen QR Token / Numeric Pin**: Viewers watching the television screen scan a rolling cryptographic QR token or input an on-screen session PIN on the mobile app (`com.zyngames.gameshow.arena`).
- **Dynamic Automated Audience-to-Squad Partitioning Algorithm**:
  - The real-time edge orchestration server automatically aggregates thousands of connected mobile participants into balanced, randomized squads or online "Families" (e.g., 5 to 10 players per team).
  - Manual player picking is prohibited to prevent collusion and ensure fair skill-based competition.
- **Sub-500ms Hivemind Aggregation Engine**:
  - For each prompt displayed on the master broadcast (survey questions, trivia, consensus buzzers), mobile devices capture individual player inputs.
  - The system computes the squad consensus / trimmed mean and relays the team's official answer directly to the TV broadcast screen in under 500ms.
- **Zero-Leak Isolated Team Voice & Tactical Chat Channels**:
  - Each randomized family/squad is provisioned an isolated WebRTC voice room and encrypted real-time chat channel.
  - Opposing teams cannot intercept or listen to opposing team strategy or answer deliberations.
- **"Hot Seat" Captain & Contestant Live Elevation**:
  - The TV show host console can spotlight random team captains or players to appear live on the TV broadcast via low-latency WebRTC video/audio feeds.

## II. GAME SHOW FORMATS & GAME ROSTER CLASSIFICATION

### A. Team / Family-Based Formats (All-Squad Consensus & Team Play)
1. **Family Feud (Crowd Feud)**:
   - *Core Mechanic*: 100-person survey reveal board with rank orders, 3 strikes buzzer system, team steals, and captain fast money rounds.
2. **The Newlywed / Squad Synergy Game ("Know Your Squad")**:
   - *Core Mechanic*: Team members must predict how their own randomly assigned teammates will answer quirky personal, moral, or trivia questions. Points are scored for high team consensus and matching answers.
3. **The Big Board Trivia Showdown (Pub Quiz / University Challenge Style)**:
   - *Core Mechanic*: Full squads huddle on multi-tier category questions. Each player submits their confidence score and answer; the team's weighted consensus is locked in against opposing families.
4. **The Price Is Right (Hivemind Bidding)**:
   - *Core Mechanic*: Squads submit estimates for showcase retail prizes. The squad's median average is submitted as the official team bid (closest without going over).

### B. Solo / Individual Gauntlets (One Player on the Hot Seat)
1. **Jeopardy! Trivia Rush**:
   - *Core Mechanic*: Category board selection ($200–$1,000 tiers), rapid buzzer timing, Daily Doubles, and Final Jeopardy wagers.
2. **1 vs. 100 (The Mob Gauntlet)**:
   - *Core Mechanic*: 1 solo contestant on the hot seat against the entire 100-person online crowd (The Mob). Wrong mob answers build the solo contestant's buyout prize pool.
3. **Wheel of Fortune (Solo Multiplier Spin)**:
   - *Core Mechanic*: Individual spinning multiplier wheel, letter calls, vowel purchases, and solve buzzers.
